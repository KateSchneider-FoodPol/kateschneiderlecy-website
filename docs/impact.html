Impact

Policy and practice

  • Indicators adopted into global monitoring frameworks. Food Systems Countdown Initiative indicators I helped design have been incorporated into the CAADP framework and into FAOSTAT, extending their use well beyond the original research.
  • Policy reports with global reach. The Food Systems Countdown Initiative’s annual assessments are released as FAO policy reports with executive summaries in six languages:
    • The state of food systems worldwide in the countdown to 2030 (2023)
    • Governance and resilience as entry points for transforming food systems (2025)
  • FAO Statistics Working Papers on employment and value added in agrifood systems, informing how national statistical offices and international agencies measure and report on the agrifood workforce. See Estimating global and country-level employment in agrifood systems.
  • USDA-funded consumer research on diet quality and food price sensitivity, supported by the Robert Wood Johnson Foundation and USDA Economic Research Service — see the USDA press release.

Open data and reproducibility

All of my empirical work is released with public replication code and data wherever possible, through GitHub and Harvard Dataverse — see each entry on the Publications page for direct links.
